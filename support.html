Support Us

Help keep PDX Hackerspace making, teaching and sharing

PDX Hackerspace is an all-volunteer, member-operated space. Everything here - the tools, the classes, the events, the day-to-day upkeep - happens because members and supporters make it happen. Your support goes directly into the space and the community.

Cash Donations

Cash donations help sponsor members, pay for repairs and upgrades, and buy materials that members use to make things with. Every dollar goes directly into the space: $25 buys a spool of 3D printer filament, $40 sponsors a full month of membership for someone who couldn't otherwise afford it, and $100 helps cover equipment repairs and upgrades.

PDX Hackerspace is a 501(c)(3) registered non-profit and all donations are tax-deductible.

Volunteer Your Time

Time and skills are just as valuable as money. Drop in at a Work Party/Open House - every Saturday except the last one of the month, from noon to 5PM. If you have a special skill that might benefit the Hackerspace, please get in touch or check out other ways to get involved.

Become a Member

The best way to support the space is to be part of it. Come to an open house for a tour and grab an application for membership. Curious about the details? Our FAQ covers how membership works.

Questions?

Have a question about donating, or something to offer that doesn't fit the categories above? Email us at donate@pdxhs.org - we'd love to hear from you.
