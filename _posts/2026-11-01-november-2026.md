## Gratitude Day: Saturday, November 28

On the Saturday after Thanksgiving, we're getting together in the back yard at the space for Gratitude Day, from 3 to 6 PM. It's for members, plus family and friends invited by a member.

Two turkeys are covered and the chafing dishes are coming out. Everything else is potluck: bring a dish to share. RSVP and tell us what you're bringing at [potluck.pdxhackerspace.org](https://potluck.pdxhackerspace.org/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.

## LED Badges

You may have seen scrolling LED name badges around the space. Members have been building on them:

- [FrogAlert](https://frogalert.org/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, open firmware that keeps the nametag, counts nearby Bluetooth devices, and shows short alerts when it detects likely police body cameras, camera glasses, or Flipper Zeros. It runs entirely on the badge and flashes from a browser.
- A [badge guide](https://badge.nyx.ms){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} covering firmware that lets newer badges work with the open-source Badge Magic app (F-Droid, Google Play, and the iOS App Store), flashing instructions, and a patched Android app.
- A [Home Assistant integration](https://github.com/romkey/hass-badgemagic){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} that sets text and animation, checks the battery, and can use a badge for notifications, plus [firmware](https://github.com/romkey/hub75-badge-magic){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} that drives two 64×32 LED panels as one large badge over Bluetooth.
- A [web designer](https://igotu.com/badge/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} for badge images and [BlinkenBadge](https://github.com/martinbogo/BlinkenBadge){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, a desktop app for Linux, macOS, and Windows.

Members can join the conversation in #nyx-badge on the member Slack. Everyone else can start at [badge.nyx.ms](https://badge.nyx.ms){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, or ask about badges at a Saturday open house.

## Craft Lab: Sewing Training

The Craft Lab's steward is finishing a Sewing Machine Basics curriculum: threading, winding a bobbin, changing needles, and choosing settings for a project, taught to up to three people at a time. A dry run with a couple of beginners is planned for a Wednesday evening in early November, and events for the workshop and a weekly Open Sew are being set up, so watch the calendar. Four or five members have offered to teach.

## Star Trek Night

Every Sunday from 5 to 8 PM, members are watching all of Star Trek: every series and every movie, four episodes a night. That works out to roughly a five-year mission.

Each night is three episodes of classic Trek in original air order, starting with the 1966 Original Series, plus one episode of modern Trek, starting with Discovery. Shows that overlapped are woven together the way they first aired, so crossovers land where they should. Fifth Sundays are flex nights for catch-up, movies, or bonus episodes. New to Trek? The order is built so nothing gets spoiled or skipped.

Star Trek Night is members only. The conversation is in #star-trek-night on the member Slack.

Patrick Stewart, who played Captain Jean-Luc Picard, died this week at 86.
