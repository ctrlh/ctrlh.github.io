Bill of Materials for PDX Hackerspace itself: the areas that make up the space, and the major assemblies and components in each. It's a high level summary, not a full inventory - for line-item detail, manuals and how-tos, check the member wiki, and for a narrative tour see Our Spaces.

This is a work in progress. Our inventory of working equipment changes dynamically. If you have questions or want to verify the availability of specific equipment, please contact us.

Electronics Lab

  • Soldering stations & hot air rework
  • Adjustable DC power supplies & variable transformer
  • Oscilloscopes
  • LA2016 USB logic analyzer
  • TinySA Ultra spectrum analyzer / function generator
  • LiteVNA64 vector network analyzer
  • Handheld multimeters
  • PCB microscopes
  • Othermill & Carvera Air CNC (PCB milling)
  • Electronic hot plate & reflow oven
  • Component inventory, managed with PartsBox
  • Loaner parts bins
  • Member project storage

Craft Lab

  • Sewing machines (White 675, Huskystar 219)
  • Serger (Janome 7933)
  • Embroidery machine (Brother SE600)
  • Vinyl cutting plotters (32" & 24")
  • Transfer tape & vinyl offcuts
  • Laminator & heat sealer
  • Coil binding machine & mat cutters
  • Tracer projector
  • Leatherworking tools
  • Classroom seating for 12, video projector & PA

Woodshop

  • Standard woodworking power & hand tools
  • 60W CO2 laser cutter
  • Shapeoko 3 XXL CNC router (33"x33" bed)
  • FLA100 CNC router (24"x24" bed)
  • Scrap wood & sheet stock for prototyping
  • Dust collection

3D Printing & Rapid Prototyping

  • 6 Prusa MK4S FDM printers
  • Prusa XL FDM printer
  • Formlabs Form 2 SLA resin printers
  • Remote print monitoring
  • Filament stock (PETG, PLA)

^H Computer Museum

  • Vintage Commodore systems
  • Vintage Apple systems
  • Vintage IBM PCs
  • 386-era and older machines

Main Space & Lounge

  • Configurable workspaces with power & Wi-Fi
  • Board games
  • Video projector
  • Hackable vending machine
  • DIY arcade cabinet
  • Loaner laptops (Acer Swift, Acer C720 Chromebook)
  • ebook server

Coworking

  • Shared desk space
  • 2D laser printers
  • Label printers
  • Receipt printer
  • Refrigerator/freezer for member day use
  • 10GbE network backbone

^H Back Space (Outdoor)

  • ~5,000 sq ft (460 sq m) of outdoor area
  • Seating areas & shade structures
  • BBQ grills
  • Raised garden beds & small orchard
  • Bike parking
  • Room for large-format projects
