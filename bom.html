Bill of Materials for PDX Hackerspace itself: the areas that make up the space, and the major assemblies and components in each. It's a high level summary, not a full inventory - for line-item detail, manuals and how-tos, check the member wiki, and for a narrative tour see Our Spaces.

This is a work in progress, and our inventory of working equipment changes dynamically. Individual machines go offline from time to time, whether they're awaiting parts, out for repair or temporarily out of service, so please confirm the status of anything you're counting on before you make the trip. Visit us during public hours or contact us to check.
